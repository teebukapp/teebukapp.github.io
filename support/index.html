Delete your account

  1. Open Teebuk and go to Settings → Account.
  2. Tap Delete account and read what will be deleted.
  3. Type the word shown to confirm, then tap Delete my account.

Your account, profile, photo, rounds and handicap are deleted straight away. You need an internet connection. If you cannot open the app, email us from the address on your account and we will delete it for you. More in the Privacy Policy.

Common questions

How is my handicap index calculated?
Teebuk follows the method published by the World Handicap System. Each eligible round gives a score differential. Once you have three, your index is worked out from your best differentials among your most recent 20 rounds. A round counts when it is a complete 18-hole round on a course whose rating, slope, pars and stroke indexes are in Teebuk. Nine-hole rounds are saved but do not count yet.

Can I score without signal?
Yes. Rounds are saved on your phone as you play and sync when you are back online. Keep the app installed and signed in until they have synced.

A course is missing, or its card is wrong.
Email us the course name and, if you can, a photo of its scorecard. We add and correct courses from real scorecards.

I have a new phone.
Install Teebuk and sign in the same way as before — the same email, Google or Apple account. Your synced rounds come back.

How do I change my name, @handle, bio or photo?
Open your profile from Home and tap Edit profile.
