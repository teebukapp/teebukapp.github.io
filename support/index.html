Help & support

Every message is read by a person.

Email us: teebukapp@gmail.com

Tell us what happened, what you expected, your phone model and the app version (Settings → About → Version). A screenshot helps. We usually reply within a few days.

Delete your account

  1. Open Teebuk and go to Settings → Account.
  2. Tap Delete account and read what will be deleted.
  3. Type the word shown to confirm, then tap Delete my account.

Your account, profile, photo, rounds and handicap, who you follow and who follows you, your blocks and reports, and your comments are deleted straight away. You need an internet connection. If you cannot open the app, email us from the address on your account and we will delete it for you. More in the Privacy Policy.

Common questions

How is my handicap index calculated?
Teebuk follows the method published by the World Handicap System. Each eligible round gives a score differential. Once you have three, your index is worked out from your best differentials among your most recent 20 rounds. A round counts when it is a complete 18-hole round on a course whose rating, slope, pars and stroke indexes are in Teebuk. Some course ratings in Teebuk are estimates (see the Terms); a round on such a course still counts, and the app marks that differential as an estimate. Nine-hole rounds are saved but do not count yet.

Can I score without signal?
Yes. Rounds are saved on your phone as you play and sync when you are back online. Keep the app installed and signed in until they have synced.

A course is missing, or its card is wrong.
Email us the course name and, if you can, a photo of its scorecard. We add and correct courses from real scorecards.

I have a new phone.
Install Teebuk and sign in the same way as before — the same email, Google or Apple account. Your synced rounds come back.

How do I change my name, @handle, bio or photo?
Open your profile from Home and tap Edit profile.

How does following work?
Search a name or @handle in People and tap Follow. You see their rounds set to Followers. If they approve followers, your request waits until they accept.

How do I choose who follows me?
Settings → Privacy → Approve followers. New followers then wait for you to accept them, under Follow requests. People who already follow you stay; to remove one, open their profile and tap ⋯ → Remove this follower. Turning Approve followers off later lets in anyone who is waiting.

How do I block or report someone?
Open their profile and tap ⋯. Blocking removes follows between you and hides you from each other; they aren’t told. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. Unblock in Settings → Privacy.

How do I delete or report a comment?
Tap ⋯ beside the comment. Delete removes your own comment, or any comment on your own round; it asks first. Report sends the comment to us to review within 24 hours, then offers to block the person who wrote it. Comments appear under rounds set to Followers once the round has synced; a Private round has none.
