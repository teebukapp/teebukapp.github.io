Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Teebuk app collects, why, who can see it, how long it is kept, and the rights you have. It applies to the Teebuk app for iPhone and Android and to this website.

1. Who we are

Teebuk is a golf scoring and handicap app operated by Kenichi Joaysa, an individual based in Thailand (“we”, “us”). We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us at teebukapp@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account. Your email address and how you sign in (an email link, Google, or Apple). If you sign in with Apple and choose to share it, we receive your name the first time you sign in. If you use Apple’s “Hide My Email”, we receive the relay address Apple gives us instead of your own.
  • Profile. Your display name, your @handle, a short bio (up to 30 characters), and a profile photo if you choose to add one. Your handicap index and tier, which we calculate from your rounds.
  • Rounds. The course and tee, its rating and slope, the score you enter for each hole, the date, time and time zone of the round, any side-game settings, and the names (and, if you enter them, the scores and course handicaps) of the people you play with. Courses you add yourself.
  • Following. Who you follow and who follows you, whether you approve new followers, and whether you appear on friends’ leaderboards. Accounts you block, and reports you send.
  • Comments. What you write under a round (up to 280 characters) and when you wrote it.
  • Clubs. If you start a club: its name, a short tag (2 to 5 characters), an optional tagline and an optional picture, and that you own it. If you are in a club or invited to one: which club, whether you are a member or only invited, and when you were invited and when you joined. If you post in a club you own: each announcement or tee time you post (an announcement’s text, up to 500 characters; for a tee time, the day and time, the course (picked from the app’s list or typed in; its name up to 100 characters) and an optional note of up to 500 characters), when it was posted and when it was edited (for a tee time, also when its day, time or course last changed), and whether it is pinned. If you are a member: your answer to a tee time, “Going” or “Not going”, and when you gave it, and the comments you write under an announcement (up to 200 characters) and when you wrote them. Reports you send about a club, an announcement or a comment: your reason, an optional note and, for an announcement or a comment, a copy of the text you reported (an announcement’s text, or for a tee time its course and note; up to 500 characters).
  • My bag. The clubs you pick for your bag and the carry distance you enter for each, and when you last saved them.
  • Questionnaire after sign-up (if you answer it). Your answers to up to 13 questions: what you most want Teebuk to help with, how long you have played golf, your usual 18-hole score, how often you play, your goal for the year, what costs you the most strokes, your favourite club, the game your golf group plays, your home course, your gender, your age range, the province you live in, and how you heard of Teebuk. Every question is optional and every answer is picked from a list: there is no free text, we do not ask your birthday, and the province is one you pick yourself, never read from your phone. We also keep when you finished the questionnaire, chose “Maybe later” or last saved it, and which version of its notice you saw.
  • Photos you pick. We only see a photo you choose from your library or take with the camera. A profile photo, or the picture of a club you own, is uploaded to our storage. A photo of a scorecard you entered, if you choose to attach one, is uploaded to private storage that only the Teebuk team can open, to check the card. A photo you place on a share card stays on your phone and is never uploaded.
  • On your phone. The app keeps your rounds, your bag, a copy of your profile and photo, your questionnaire answers, your settings and your sign-in session on the device, so it works without a connection.
  • App updates. Each time the app opens, Expo’s update service receives a random id for this installation, the app version, the error message if the app failed to open last time, and your IP address (section 5). It is not linked to your account.

What we do not collect. Your phone’s location, the contacts or address book on your phone, microphone, health data, advertising identifiers, browsing history or payment details. Teebuk has no analytics or advertising tools from other companies, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell your data. The one outside tool in the app that hears about its launches and launch errors is Expo’s update service (section 5): it receives a random id for the installation, the error message after a failed launch and your IP address, and counts launches and failed launches for each update.

3. Why we use it

  • To provide Teebuk — your account, saving and syncing your rounds, calculating your handicap, showing your profile and running the clubs you start or join. This is necessary to provide the service you asked for.
  • Your profile photo — only because you chose to add one. You can remove it at any time.
  • A photo of a scorecard — only if you attach one to a card you entered, to check that card. Only the Teebuk team sees it, and it is deleted with the round or your account.
  • To answer you when you contact us, to keep the service secure and working, and to keep it safe for other players — reviewing reports, and hiding content or suspending accounts that break our Terms. These are our legitimate interests.
  • To understand how Teebuk is used and decide what to improve. On our own servers we count totals from the data described in section 2: when accounts were created and whether the sign-in was confirmed, the rounds you play and their settings (course, tee, holes, date, whether the card is complete, Private or Followers), how many players are on a card (never their names), side games, whether you have a handicap index, who follows whom, which rounds have comments and by whom (never the text), blocks between a round’s owner and a commenter (read only to leave such comments out), and which courses and cards are played. For example: how many players finished a round this month, how many new players played a second round within 30 days, how often a side game is used, and whether two accounts played the same course at about the same time. Accounts suspended or comments hidden for breaking the Terms, and our own test accounts, are left out. We look only at totals that cover at least 10 players, never publish or share a figure about one person, and count only the data listed above, which we already hold to run the app (answers to the questionnaire, which you choose whether to give, are covered in the next item). This is our legitimate interest. You can leave these counts at any time in Settings → Privacy → Count me in Teebuk’s statistics, or by email (section 7); it takes effect from then on.
  • The questionnaire — only if you answer it. Your answers are used for two things. First, three of them set up the app for you: your home course comes first when you start a round, the tees for the gender you chose come first, and your starting target score is set near the score you usually shoot when we have nothing better to go on. Second, we count them as totals that cover at least 10 players, never one person’s answers, to learn who uses Teebuk and what to build next. Your answer to how you heard about Teebuk is also counted together with whether you played a second round within 30 days of your first, again only as totals over at least 10 players, to learn which channels bring players who keep playing. No screen in the app shows your answers to anyone else, and the score you give never touches your handicap or any leaderboard. If you turn off Settings → Privacy → Count me in Teebuk’s statistics, your answers are left out of these totals too. The legal basis is your consent. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Questionnaire → Clear all answers, and the app then goes back to its usual setup; you can change any single answer on the same screen.

We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

4. Who can see your data

  • Your profile — your display name, @handle, bio, profile photo, and handicap index and tier — can be seen by other signed-in Teebuk users. Signed-in users can also see who you follow and who follows you. Your profile photo is stored at a web address that cannot be guessed, but anyone who has that address can open the image.
  • Your rounds. You choose “Private” or “Followers” for each round (Settings → Privacy sets the default). A Private round is seen only by you and by any playing partner linked to their Teebuk account on that round. A round set to Followers can be opened in full — every hole, and the names and scores of the people you played with — by everyone who follows you. You can change a round between Private and Followers at any time from the round itself, including after it has been posted; followers see the change once the round next syncs. If you turn on Approve followers, new followers must wait for you to accept them; people who already follow you keep following until you remove them. In the app, a name that contains a word we don’t allow is shown to others as “Player”.
  • Leaderboard. People who follow you see you on their leaderboard: your handicap index and tier, your best 18-hole score this month with the course it was played on, how far your 18-hole round this month that came furthest under your handicap (as it stood before that round) came under or over it, with its course, and how many rounds you played this month. Only rounds set to Followers count. To leave every leaderboard, turn off Settings → Privacy → Appear on friends’ leaderboards.
  • Comments. A comment you write under a round can be seen by everyone who can open that round: its owner and, when the round is set to Followers, the owner’s followers and any playing partner linked to their Teebuk account on that round. The comments on a Private round are seen only by its owner. Your display name, @handle and profile photo show beside it. You can delete your own comments, and a round’s owner can delete any comment on their round. Comments from accounts you block, or that block you, are hidden from you, and yours from them. When you block someone, their comments on your rounds, and yours on their rounds, are hidden from everyone until you unblock them. You can report any comment from the ⋯ beside it; a report keeps a copy of the comment’s text for review, even if the comment is deleted afterwards. In the app’s Notifications, a round’s owner is told when someone comments on it, and people who commented earlier are told when the owner comments after them.
  • Clubs. Everyone in a club sees everyone else in it on the club’s leaderboard, whether or not they follow each other: their handicap index and tier, their best 18-hole score this month with the course it was played on, how far their 18-hole round this month that came furthest under their handicap (as it stood before that round) came under or over it, with its course, and how many rounds they played this month. These are the same numbers as the friends’ leaderboard, and only rounds set to Followers count; a Private round never does. If you turn off Settings → Privacy → Appear on friends’ leaderboards, you are left off a club’s board too. Members see the member list, and a club’s owner also sees who has been invited. Only the club’s members see its announcements and tee times, who has answered “Going” or “Not going” to a tee time and who has not answered yet, and the comments under an announcement; the name, @handle and profile photo of whoever posted, answered or commented show beside it. A comment or answer from a player you blocked, or who blocked you, is hidden from you, and that player is left out of the lists of who is going, not going or has not answered that you see. A comment or answer from a player who has blocked the club’s owner, or whom the owner has blocked, is hidden from everyone. An announcement or comment that the Teebuk team has hidden is hidden from everyone, and hiding an announcement hides its comments and answers too. A report you send about an announcement or a comment is seen only by you and the Teebuk team. Anyone signed in can see a club’s cover (its picture, name, tag, tagline, how many members it has, and its owner’s name and @handle), and the tag of the club a person belongs to appears after their name on their profile and in people search. A club’s picture is stored at a web address that cannot be guessed, but anyone who has that address can open the image. If a club’s owner has blocked another player, or that player has blocked the owner, that player does not see the club’s cover or tag.
  • Playing partners. Naming someone in a round does not give them access to it; they see it only if they follow you and the round is set to Followers. The one exception is a playing partner linked to their Teebuk account on that round: they can open it whether it is set to Private or Followers, even if they do not follow you, and when it is set to Followers they also see its comments and can comment.
  • Reports. If you report someone, only you and Teebuk see the report, to review it. The person you report is not told who reported them.
  • Courses you add are visible only to you.
  • Your bag is visible only to you.
  • Your questionnaire answers are visible only to you; we look only at totals (section 3).
  • Share cards go only where you send them, through your phone’s share sheet.
  • Sponsored banners on the Home screen are pictures built into the app. We share no personal data with sponsors. We may tell a sponsor overall figures, such as how many players use Teebuk, that cover at least 10 players and say nothing about any one person. If a banner has a link, tapping it opens the sponsor’s website in your browser, under that site’s own privacy policy.

5. Service providers and where your data is stored

  • Supabase provides our database, sign-in and file storage. Your data is stored on its servers in Singapore. Its server logs are stored in the European Union; its staff in the United States may access data to give us technical support; and profile photos and club pictures, which anyone with the address can open, may be served through a worldwide content network. Supabase processes your data only on our instructions, under a data processing agreement that includes the EU standard contractual clauses. Storing it there is necessary to provide the service to you.
  • Google or Apple, only if you choose to sign in with them. They handle that sign-in under their own privacy policies.
  • Apple and Google distribute the app through their stores, and your phone’s operating system provides the share sheet.
  • Expo delivers app updates. Each time the app opens on a phone, it asks Expo’s update service whether a newer version of the app’s code is ready, and downloads it if so. That request carries a random number the app makes for this installation (it is not tied to your account or to your phone’s hardware, and it changes if you reinstall the app), the platform (iOS or Android), the app version, which update is running, and, if the app failed to open last time, the technical error message; like any connection, it also gives Expo your IP address. No account details, rounds, names or anything else you enter is sent. Expo uses this to send the right update and to count, for us, how many installations opened each update and how often it failed to open. Expo, a company in the United States, processes this on our behalf under its own privacy policy and keeps it on its own schedule. The website does not use it.
  • Server and sign-in logs. Like any online service, our host keeps routine server and sign-in logs (your IP address, the time and the request) for security and troubleshooting. They are kept for a short period on Supabase’s schedule and are not used to analyse you.
  • This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts; both receive your IP address when you open a page. The site sets no cookies. One page (/u/, which opens a shared profile in the app) runs a small script that reads the @handle from the link; nothing tracks you.

6. How long we keep it

  • We keep your data for as long as you have an account.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. Deletion takes effect immediately. Your account, profile, photo, rounds, handicap, bag and the courses you added are deleted from our servers, and the app removes them from that phone. Who you follow and who follows you, blocks you made or that were made against you, and reports you sent or that were made about you are deleted too. So are the comments you wrote, and every comment on your rounds. Your questionnaire answers are deleted as well, from our servers and from that phone.
  • Clubs. Leaving a club or being removed from it deletes your membership, but the comments and answers you left there stay, under your name. A block between you and the club’s owner, made by either of you, deletes your membership or invite, and declining an invite, or the owner cancelling it, deletes the invite. Announcements, tee times, answers and comments stay until they are deleted, even after a tee time has passed. Changing a tee time’s day, time or course deletes all its answers, so that members answer again; changing only its note keeps them. Deleting an announcement deletes its answers and comments. You can delete your own comments while you are in the club, and a club’s owner can delete any comment in their club; after you leave, ask us (section 7) to delete yours. Deleting a club deletes its memberships, invites, picture, announcements, answers and comments. If you delete your account, your membership or invite in any club goes too, and so do your comments, your answers, and the announcements and tee times you posted, even in a club you have since handed over to someone else, together with the answers and comments under them; a club you own is deleted with it, with its member list, picture, announcements, answers and comments. A report about an announcement or a comment keeps its own copy of the reported text after the announcement, the comment or the club is deleted, and is deleted when either account is deleted, as described above. A copy of your data, if you ask for one (section 7), lists the clubs you own, your club memberships and invites, the announcements and tee times you posted, the comments you wrote under club announcements and your answers to tee times.
  • Questionnaire answers are kept until you clear them (Settings → Questionnaire → Clear all answers) or delete your account. Clearing removes every answer from our servers and from that phone at once; we keep only when you finished the questionnaire, chose “Maybe later” or last saved it, and which version of its notice you saw, so that it does not ask you again. That you saw it, and whether you finished it or chose “Maybe later”, also stays in the totals of how many players did each (totals over at least 10 players, never one person’s answers). To be left out of those too, turn off Settings → Privacy → Count me in Teebuk’s statistics.
  • The update check is the exception. What Expo receives when the app checks for updates (section 5) is not linked to your account, so deleting your account does not remove it; Expo keeps it on its own schedule. Deleting the app stops it, and a new install gets a new id.
  • After deletion, technical sync records that hold no content, only identifiers, are removed within 30 days. Copies in our hosting provider’s routine backups are removed as those backups are replaced.
  • Totals we have already counted for statistics (section 3), for example how many players played in a month, hold no one’s identity and cover at least 10 players. They are kept after an account is deleted, for as long as Teebuk runs.
  • If you were a playing partner in someone else’s round, your name as they typed it stays in their round, because it is their record.
  • Signing out without deleting keeps your rounds on that phone, so they are there when you sign back in.

7. Your rights

Under Thailand’s Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) you have the right to:

  • access your personal data and get a copy of it;
  • correct it — most of it you can edit in the app;
  • delete it — in the app, as described above;
  • receive it in a commonly used format, or have it sent to another provider — email us;
  • object to or restrict how we use it — for the statistics in section 3, turn off Settings → Privacy → Count me in Teebuk’s statistics, and your account is left out of every count from then on (totals already counted are not changed, except the questionnaire totals: they are not stored but counted again each time they are looked at, so you are left out of them from the next count);
  • withdraw your consent for your profile photo, by removing it;
  • withdraw your consent for the questionnaire, by clearing your answers in Settings → Questionnaire → Clear all answers (the questionnaire totals are not stored: they are counted again each time they are looked at, so your answers are left out of them from the next count);
  • complain to the Office of the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC) of Thailand.

To make a request, email teebukapp@gmail.com from the address on your account. We reply within 30 days and may ask you to confirm that the account is yours.

What Expo holds from the update check (section 5) is not linked to your account, and the app never reads the installation’s id, so we cannot look it up for you. You can ask Expo directly under its privacy policy (expo.dev/privacy), or delete the app.

8. Security

All connections between the app and our servers are encrypted (HTTPS). Access rules on our database let each account change only its own data. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Children

Teebuk is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
