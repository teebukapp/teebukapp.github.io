Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Teebuk app collects, why, who can see it, how long it is kept, and the rights you have. It applies to the Teebuk app for iPhone and Android and to this website.

1. Who we are

Teebuk is a golf scoring and handicap app operated by Kenichi Joaysa, an individual based in Thailand (“we”, “us”). We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us at teebukapp@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account. Your email address and how you sign in (an email link, Google, or Apple). If you sign in with Apple and choose to share it, we receive your name the first time you sign in. If you use Apple’s “Hide My Email”, we receive the relay address Apple gives us instead of your own.
  • Profile. Your display name, your @handle, a short bio (up to 30 characters), and a profile photo if you choose to add one. Your handicap index and tier, which we calculate from your rounds.
  • Rounds. The course and tee, its rating and slope, the score you enter for each hole, the date, time and time zone of the round, any side-game settings, and the names (and, if you enter them, the scores and course handicaps) of the people you play with. Courses you add yourself.
  • Following. Who you follow and who follows you, whether you approve new followers, and whether you appear on friends’ leaderboards. Accounts you block, and reports you send.
  • Photos you pick. We only see a photo you choose from your library or take with the camera. A profile photo is uploaded to our storage. A photo of a scorecard you entered, if you choose to attach one, is uploaded to private storage that only the Teebuk team can open, to check the card. A photo you place on a share card stays on your phone and is never uploaded.
  • On your phone. The app keeps your rounds, a copy of your profile and photo, your settings and your sign-in session on the device, so it works without a connection.

What we do not collect. Your location, contacts, microphone, health data, advertising identifiers, browsing history or payment details. Teebuk has no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising tools, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell your data.

3. Why we use it

  • To provide Teebuk — your account, saving and syncing your rounds, calculating your handicap and showing your profile. This is necessary to provide the service you asked for.
  • Your profile photo — only because you chose to add one. You can remove it at any time.
  • A photo of a scorecard — only if you attach one to a card you entered, to check that card. Only the Teebuk team sees it, and it is deleted with the round or your account.
  • To answer you when you contact us, to keep the service secure and working, and to keep it safe for other players — reviewing reports, and hiding content or suspending accounts that break our Terms. These are our legitimate interests.

We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

4. Who can see your data

  • Your profile — your display name, @handle, bio, profile photo, and handicap index and tier — can be seen by other signed-in Teebuk users. Signed-in users can also see who you follow and who follows you. Your profile photo is stored at a web address that cannot be guessed, but anyone who has that address can open the image.
  • Your rounds. You choose “Private” or “Followers” for each round (Settings → Privacy sets the default). A Private round is seen only by you. A round set to Followers can be opened in full — every hole, and the names and scores of the people you played with — by everyone who follows you. If you turn on Approve followers, new followers must wait for you to accept them; people who already follow you keep following until you remove them. In the app, a name that contains a word we don’t allow is shown to others as “Player”.
  • Leaderboard. People who follow you see you on their leaderboard: your handicap index and tier, your best 18-hole score this month with the course it was played on, and how many rounds you played this month. Only rounds set to Followers count. To leave every leaderboard, turn off Settings → Privacy → Appear on friends’ leaderboards.
  • Playing partners. Naming someone in a round does not give them access to it; they see it only if they follow you and the round is set to Followers.
  • Reports. If you report someone, only you and Teebuk see the report, to review it. The person you report is not told who reported them.
  • Courses you add are visible only to you.
  • Share cards go only where you send them, through your phone’s share sheet.
  • Sponsored banners on the Home screen are pictures built into the app. We share no data with sponsors. If a banner has a link, tapping it opens the sponsor’s website in your browser, under that site’s own privacy policy.

5. Service providers and where your data is stored

  • Supabase provides our database, sign-in and file storage. Your data is stored on its servers in Singapore, and Supabase processes it only on our instructions. Storing it there is necessary to provide the service to you.
  • Google or Apple, only if you choose to sign in with them. They handle that sign-in under their own privacy policies.
  • Apple and Google distribute the app through their stores, and your phone’s operating system provides the share sheet.
  • Server and sign-in logs. Like any online service, our host keeps routine server and sign-in logs (your IP address, the time and the request) for security and troubleshooting. They are kept for a short period on Supabase’s schedule and are not used to analyse you.
  • This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts; both receive your IP address when you open a page. The site sets no cookies. One page (/u/, which opens a shared profile in the app) runs a small script that reads the @handle from the link; nothing tracks you.

6. How long we keep it

  • We keep your data for as long as you have an account.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. Deletion takes effect immediately. Your account, profile, photo, rounds, handicap and the courses you added are deleted from our servers, and the app removes them from that phone. Who you follow and who follows you, blocks you made or that were made against you, and reports you sent or that were made about you are deleted too.
  • After deletion, technical sync records that hold no content, only identifiers, are removed within 30 days. Copies in our hosting provider’s routine backups are removed as those backups are replaced.
  • If you were a playing partner in someone else’s round, your name as they typed it stays in their round, because it is their record.
  • Signing out without deleting keeps your rounds on that phone, so they are there when you sign back in.

7. Your rights

Under Thailand’s Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) you have the right to:

  • access your personal data and get a copy of it;
  • correct it — most of it you can edit in the app;
  • delete it — in the app, as described above;
  • receive it in a commonly used format, or have it sent to another provider — email us;
  • object to or restrict how we use it;
  • withdraw your consent for your profile photo, by removing it;
  • complain to the Office of the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC) of Thailand.

To make a request, email teebukapp@gmail.com from the address on your account. We reply within 30 days and may ask you to confirm that the account is yours.

8. Security

All connections between the app and our servers are encrypted (HTTPS). Access rules on our database let each account change only its own data. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Children

Teebuk is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
